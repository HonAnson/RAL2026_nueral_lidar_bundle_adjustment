\section{Introduction}

Recently, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) have gained significant interest for their powerful capabilities in scene reconstruction beyond their original application for novel view synthesis. While being initially developed for RGB images (RGB NeRFs), recent research has extended NeRF to LiDAR point clouds (LiDAR NeRFs), including LiDAR odometry~\cite{deng2023nerfloam} and novel view synthesis~\cite{zheng2024lidar4D}. However, it is still an open question of how to effectively leverage NeRFs for LiDAR 3D mapping and reconstruction, which is a long-standing and fundamental problem in robotics and computer vision with critical applications in LiDAR odometry and Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM).

A major advancement in RGB NeRF research is NeRF Bundle Adjustment (NeRF-BA)~\cite{lin2021barf,wang2022nerfmm,chen2023l2gnerf}, which leverages the differentiability of camera projection to jointly optimize the scene neural representation and camera poses. This has demonstrated remarkable success in recovering accurate camera poses from noisy pose initialization. Nonetheless, few studies have explicitly focused on the problem of LiDAR NeRF-BA, and the fundamental differences in the designing of RGB NeRFs and LiDAR NeRFs remain underexplored. 

Contrary to RGB images, which contain rich texture for camera pose optimization in RGB NeRF-BA, LiDAR point clouds post the following challenges for LiDAR NeRF-BA: (1) LiDAR point clouds inherently lack textures. A direct implementation of RGB NeRF-BA on LiDAR point clouds would result in sub-optimal pose optimization, as it overlooks the strong geometric cue, i.e., accurate ranges, from LiDAR rays. (2) LiDAR point clouds are spatially sparse. Directly using the volume sampling strategy in RGB NeRFs would result in inefficient volume sampling and hinder pose optimization, which will be discussed in Section~\ref{sec:volume_sampling}.

In this work, we study the underlying characteristics of NeRF volume sampling and leverage it to address the LiDAR NeRF-BA problem. Given an initially noisy estimation of LiDAR poses in a sequence, a novel LiDAR NeRF-BA algorithm, termed NeLD-BA, is proposed to jointly reconstruct scenes and refine LiDAR poses, beyond the capabilities of existing LiDAR NeRF methods on novel view synthesis and odometry (e.g., Figure~\ref{fig:intro_example}). We investigate and show that LiDAR NeRF-BA performance is closely coupled with the density of volume sampling. %Specifically, it is found out that pose optimization is explicitly enforced by densely sampled volume points. 
Therefore, a novel volume sampling method tailored for LiDAR ray models is proposed to enhance LiDAR NeRF-BA. Furthermore, we tackle the challenge of gradient instability caused by positional encoding~\cite{lin2021barf} by introducing surrogate gradients. Finally, inspired by DS-NeRF~\cite{deng2024dsnerf}, we design a loss function for LiDAR point clouds that enhances reconstruction quality and pose accuracy simultaneously.

The main contributions of this work include: 1) We provide new insights that pose accuracy in LiDAR NeRF-BA is explicitly reinforced by volume sampling positions; 2) Novel, simple, yet efficient strategies are proposed for volume sampling and positional encoding tailored for LiDAR NeRF-BA with a LiDAR-specific loss function; 3) Extensive experiments are conducted against state-of-the-art algorithms, showing superior mapping and localization performance.

We will open-source our code for the community upon the acceptance of the paper.
