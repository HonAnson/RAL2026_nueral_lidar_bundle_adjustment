\section{Conclusions}
This work delves into the mechanics of LiDAR NeRF-BA, showing that volume sampling directly governs the pose gradient, and accordingly proposes pre-termination fine sampling, which densely sample the sharp density transition at the surface to improve pose gradient, together with a surrogate gradient and a termination distribution loss that further enhance BA quality. A promising direction for future work is to explore hash grid features or other representations such as SDFs for LiDAR BA. Finally, we will open-source the source code of our work to benefit the community.
